# ACME Robotics — Employee Handbook (FICTIONAL sample document for evals)

ACME Robotics is a fictional company. Nothing in this document is real.

## Leave

Full-time employees accrue 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave accrues monthly and unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried into
the following year; anything above 5 days is forfeited on 31 December.

Employees are entitled to 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required
only for a sick absence longer than two consecutive working days.

Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks at
full pay for the secondary caregiver.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Working remotely for four
or more days per week requires written approval from the employee's director and
is reviewed every six months.

The company does not reimburse home internet or electricity costs. It provides a
one-time 300 dollar home-office equipment stipend to every new employee.

## Expenses

Expense claims must be submitted within 60 days of the expense being incurred.
Claims submitted after 60 days are not reimbursed except in documented emergencies.

Any single expense above 500 dollars requires director sign-off before the expense
is incurred. Travel booked less than 14 days in advance requires the same sign-off.

The daily meal allowance while travelling is 60 dollars domestic and 90 dollars
international. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Equipment and security

Every employee receives a company laptop. Laptops are refreshed every three years.
Personal devices may not be used to access production systems.

Full-disk encryption and the company password manager are mandatory on all
company laptops. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT Security within
24 hours.

Multi-factor authentication is required for all company accounts. Passwords must
be at least 14 characters.

## Probation and notice

New employees serve a probation period of six months. During probation the notice
period is two weeks. After probation the notice period is one month for employees
and three months for managers.

Performance reviews are held twice per year, in June and December.
